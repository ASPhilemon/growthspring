Mission

Improve members’ socio-economic welfare through collective saving and investing, with flexible, accessible credit—so every member thrives.

Objectives

  • Encourage disciplined saving and investing.
  • Provide easy access to credit.
  • Offer support to members in times of need.
  • Foster unity, cooperation, and professional networking.
  • Share guidance to improve members’ economic welfare.
  • Invest pooled funds and manage group assets prudently.
Chapter 03

Club Regulations

Saving & Points

Monthly minimum 20k. No penalties; Points are gained for saving—3 points per 10k saved. Points can be transferred or sold (except the initial 500), or used to pay interest on loans.

Profits & Withdrawals

Deposits are withdrawn only when exiting the Club. Profits are distributed annually; members choose to re-invest or withdraw.

Loans

2% per month interest on remaining balance. Loan limit: 2× savings minus unpaid loans. Max duration 2 years (smaller loans under 1 year). After month 6, points clear interest. Guarantors required beyond savings for new members.

Meetings

Held twice a year; members attend at least one (in person or online).
